AI agents and automations are only as good as what they know, and most of them have no idea
what happened in the world this morning. Follo fixes that. You tell it what matters, and it does
the monitoring for you.

## How it works

1. **Define topics.** Describe anything in plain language: a competitor, a market, a technology
   or a trend.
2. **Filter the noise.** Follo watches news outlets, industry blogs, press releases and social
   media, and scores every article for relevance, so only what matters gets through.
3. **Push events.** Relevant events fire webhooks into n8n, Make, Slack or any other tool in
   your stack. There's no polling or scheduling to set up.
4. **Or pull context.** Each topic builds a living timeline of key events, which you can fetch
   from a digest API or inject into an agent's memory before it runs.

Follo is open for early access, and you can set up your first topic without a credit card.
